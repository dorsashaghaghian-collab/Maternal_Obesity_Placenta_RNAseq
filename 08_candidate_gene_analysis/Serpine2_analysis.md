## Biological Function

Serpine2 (Serpin Family E Member 2) encodes a secreted serine protease inhibitor involved in extracellular matrix regulation and tissue remodeling.

## Relevance to Placental Biology

Placental development requires extensive extracellular matrix remodeling, vascular regulation, and trophoblast migration.

Altered Serpine2 expression may indicate changes in extracellular matrix-related processes under maternal obesity conditions.

## Hypothesis

Downregulation of Serpine2 in maternal obesity placenta may contribute to altered extracellular matrix organization and placental functional adaptation.

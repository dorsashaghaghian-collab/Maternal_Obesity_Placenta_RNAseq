# MA Plot

The MA plot was generated from DESeq2 differential expression analysis of mouse placental RNA-seq data.

The x-axis represents mean gene expression levels (baseMean), and the y-axis represents log2 fold change.

Genes with larger deviations from zero indicate stronger expression changes between maternal obesity and control groups.

Serpine2 was identified as a significantly downregulated gene:
- log2FC: -1.526
- adjusted p-value: 0.011
